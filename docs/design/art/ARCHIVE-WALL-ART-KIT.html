檔案櫃 8×8 美術包

文件庫「檔案櫃」的新尋物場景：8×8 共 64 個抽屜，每開 8 個抽屜一場查勤戰。這份包含需要生成的圖、參考縮圖與 prompt。圖由你生成，不由 Claude 代畫。

順序圖輸出檔名
1B　抽屜正面（關閉）512×512 透明底 PNG，runtime 限制 100KB 以內archive-drawer-closed-a／-b／-c（三個變體，只有磨損不同）
2C　抽屜正面（拉開）512×512 透明底 PNG，100KB 以內archive-drawer-pulled
3A　檔案牆底板1536×864，runtime 限制 600KB 以內archive-wall-plate
4D　查勤戰前場景圖（1 張共用）1536×864，600KB 以內archive-patrol-prebattle

流程

  1. 依順序生成：B → C → A → D（C 要附 B 的成品，D 要附 A 的成品）。
  2. 每張依「參考圖」順序附上縮圖旁標示的現有檔案，再貼 prompt。
  3. 生成好的檔案請放進 Game_claude，我再轉檔、量出 A 的凹面位置、登記並接進遊戲。
  4. 現有抽屜近景（archive-drawer-closeup）與卷宗（archive-folder-v2）沿用，不用重畫。

沿用的現有圖

抽屜近景、卷宗、紙張。原本的 archive-drawer-front 是 3.5:1 的長條，與 8×8 方格不合，所以重畫成方形（B）。

B　抽屜正面（關閉）

用途
程式把它複製 64 次排成 8×8 格。三個變體隨機混用，避免 64 格看起來一模一樣。
輸出
512×512 透明底 PNG，runtime 限制 100KB 以內
檔名
archive-drawer-closed-a／-b／-c（三個變體，只有磨損不同）

參考圖（依序附上）

Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Single transparent game prop asset, detailed realistic dark-fantasy, deep green and antique gold palette, restrained lantern warmth, same painterly style, lighting and colour grade as the existing story paintings. Same riveted dark-green painted steel with worn antique-gold edge trim and brass fittings as reference1 (existing drawer front) and reference2 (existing cabinet body). ONE closed small card-catalogue-style steel drawer front seen straight-on, orthographic and flat, SQUARE proportions (1:1), filling about 94% of the 512x512 canvas with an identical thin transparent margin on all four sides, centred. A slightly raised square panel with one small rivet at each of the four corners; a small EMPTY rectangular brass label-holder frame in the upper third (the label area inside is dark, flat and completely blank); one compact heavy brass pull handle centred in the lower third. Clean even light from above, subtle wear and fine scratches, understated and heavy rather than ornate. The silhouette must be a perfectly straight square with sharp clean edges so 64 copies tile edge to edge in an 8x8 grid. NO letters, numbers, readable text, title, UI, logo or watermark anywhere, no markings on the label frame, no crystals, no wooden parts, no hands, no papers sticking out. Genuine transparent alpha outside the drawer silhouette, no floor, no backdrop, no cast shadow halo. Produce three variants with exactly the same silhouette, panel layout, label frame and handle position, differing ONLY in surface wear: variant A cleanest, with the lightest scratches; variant B more worn gold edges and a few darker stains; variant C a faint shallow dent near one corner and slightly duller brass.

三個變體請分三次生成，同一張參考圖，最後一句換成 variant A／B／C。輪廓與把手位置必須一致，否則格子會對不齊。

A　檔案牆底板

用途
尋物畫面的背景。中央留一塊完全空白的方形凹面，程式在上面疊 64 個抽屜（B/C）。因為抽屜是獨立素材，縮放拖曳時抽屜依然清晰。
輸出
1536×864，runtime 限制 600KB 以內
檔名
archive-wall-plate

參考圖（依序附上）

Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Nightfall Duel wide 1536x864 background plate for a hidden-object wall, detailed realistic dark-fantasy, deep green and antique gold palette, restrained lantern warmth, same painterly style, lighting and colour grade as the existing story paintings, same materials as reference1 (the archive hall) and references 2 and 3 (riveted dark-green steel cabinet with worn antique-gold trim). Straight-on, orthographic, symmetrical view of one gigantic floor-to-ceiling steel filing wall filling the whole frame. In the exact centre sits a perfectly SQUARE, flat, recessed backing panel of plain dark green-black steel, about 830x830 px (96% of the frame height), with razor-straight edges, surrounded by a thick riveted steel frame with antique-gold edge trim. The panel interior must be COMPLETELY EMPTY and nearly uniform: no drawers, no grid lines, no handles, no labels, no shelves, no objects, no papers, because 64 drawer sprites will be overlaid by code. To the left and right of the frame, the steel wall continues with a few riveted vertical girders and shallow shadow, one wall lantern at the upper left and one at the upper right casting warm pools of light, faint dust in the air, cool shadow at the bottom edge. No characters, no hands, no crystals, no wooden parts, NO letters, numbers, readable text, title, UI, logo or watermark anywhere.

中央方形凹面的大小與位置我會量出來對齊抽屜格，所以邊緣要乾淨、不要有斜角或透視。
